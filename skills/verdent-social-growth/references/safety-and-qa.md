# Safety and Publication QA

## Hard failures

Do not publish when any item fails:

- official launch state or access state is unknown but copy says the feature/model is live
- a confidential, embargoed, or hidden detail appears in copy or media
- a performance, cost, benchmark, or comparison claim lacks an approved source
- copy implies access to or analysis of individual user data without explicit approval
- multiple unrelated releases are presented as one launch without confirmation
- a partner announcement, mention, quote, or logo use is not coordinated
- the visible demo contradicts the copy

## Claim rules

- Attribute provider specifications when useful.
- Preserve qualifiers such as `approximately`, `up to`, `limited preview`, and test conditions.
- Do not convert `2x cheaper` into a different mathematical claim. Prefer the exact approved wording or a precise, reviewed alternative.
- Never add numbers because the format asks for specificity.
- Treat superlatives, `state of the art`, benchmark leadership, and competitor comparisons as high-risk claims.
- Do not imply a provider's general model claim is a measured result inside Verdent.

## Privacy rules

Reject language such as:

- `we looked at user data`
- `our data shows`
- `we analyzed user sessions`
- `we tracked user behavior`
- any wording implying private prompt, code, workspace, or behavioral analysis

Allowed sources still require truth and approval: user interviews, volunteered feedback, support themes, public submissions, internal dogfooding, and approved aggregate research.

## Style rules

- Use clear native English for public copy.
- Do not use em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
- Avoid: `game-changer`, `revolutionary`, `unleash`, `seamlessly`, `excited to share`, `thrilled`, `delighted`, `cutting-edge`, `best-in-class`, `leverage` in the corporate sense, and `synergy`.
- Avoid invented urgency, fake scarcity, and generic excitement.
- Prefer direct verbs: supports, adds, improves, lets, helps, reduces, finds, edits, searches.

## Platform checks

- X short post is at most 280 characters, including labels and links.
- Reddit has a separate title and body.
- Instagram/TikTok hashtags are relevant and limited, not a fixed mandatory block.
- Discord mass mention is intentional.
- CTA destination exists and matches the reader action.
- Account name, model capitalization, version, and provider are correct.

## Final result

Return pass/fail by category, not an arbitrary total score:

- Facts: PASS/FAIL
- Public scope: PASS/FAIL
- Claims: PASS/FAIL
- Privacy: PASS/FAIL
- Platform fit: PASS/FAIL
- Language/style: PASS/FAIL
- Assets: PASS/FAIL/NOT APPLICABLE
- Final approval: PENDING/APPROVED

`READY` requires every applicable hard category to pass and final approval to be approved.
