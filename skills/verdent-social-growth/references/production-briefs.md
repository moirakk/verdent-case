# Production Briefs

## Poster brief

Provide:

- asset type and target platforms
- version/model/feature name
- provider, when relevant
- main title, ideally 3 to 8 words
- supporting line, ideally under 18 words
- one featured capability
- access badge: GA, Early Access, Limited Preview, Limited-time Free, or none
- screenshot, provider logo, and Verdent brand assets required
- content to hide or crop
- required sizes and delivery deadline

Do not place a changelog on the poster. The visual should answer: what is it, and why should the user care?

## Video brief

Provide a table with:

- time range
- picture/action
- voiceover
- on-screen text
- edit instruction
- privacy or hiding instruction

Mark:

- opening result shot
- essential workflow steps
- loading or waiting to cut or accelerate
- sensitive workspace, account, code, prompts, or user information to hide
- zoom, cursor emphasis, annotation, and subtitle moments
- end card and CTA
- platform ratios and duration variants

Do not write a voiceover claim that the visible product flow does not demonstrate.

## Recommended outputs

- X / LinkedIn landscape: 16:9 or platform-approved current size
- Instagram feed: 4:5; Reel: 9:16
- TikTok: 9:16
- Always confirm current template dimensions with the design owner before final export.

## Handoff gate

Before sending to design or editing:

- final spelling and official capitalization are confirmed
- screenshots match the release candidate
- model/provider logos are official
- access badge is confirmed
- hidden regions are explicitly marked
- product owner has reviewed the selected workflow
