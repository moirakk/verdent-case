# Observed Verdent Voice Baseline

Baseline date: 2026-07-27. Refresh the latest-two layer before material campaigns or after meaningful account changes.

## Evidence quality

- X and Reddit: current first-party post pages were publicly readable.
- LinkedIn: the official page and one current first-party post page were publicly indexed. The live post feed required sign-in, so the second sample is the newest publicly indexed official update.
- Discord, Instagram, and TikTok: two current first-party posts were not publicly readable. Do not present their account-specific guidance as observed fact.

## Latest-two rule

For every requested platform, use the two newest verifiable Verdent-owned posts as the strongest current evidence for voice and formatting. Record their dates or order, URLs, and content types before writing.

Use them to calibrate:

- naming and release-state wording
- opening shape and paragraph length
- amount of capability detail
- CTA, emoji, hashtags, and relationship to the asset

Do not use an old post as proof for a new model capability, product state, number, comparison, or public boundary. If the current task type differs from both samples, preserve the current mode's message architecture and borrow only neutral surface patterns.

## Rules deliberately not inherited from the previous writer

- no universal `engagement over impressions` objective
- no unsupported platform ranking weights
- no requirement to make every post controversial
- no requirement to end every formal announcement with a question
- no invented numbers or benchmarks
- no mandatory fixed hashtag count across all topics
- no previous employee machine paths, Feishu credentials, or cleanup commands
- no visual style that conflicts with the maintained Verdent design system

## How to improve this baseline

Export at least the last 20 posts per platform with date, content type, format, asset, views/impressions, comments, shares/reposts, saves when available, and CTA. Analyze by comparable content type. Promote a pattern into this Skill only after it repeats across several relevant posts.
